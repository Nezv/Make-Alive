# campanile — the town hall's campanile (S214)

Family: `tools/models/props/campanile.py` · registry: `scripts/models/elements.gd` · build: `tools\blender.ps1 tools\models\props.py campanile` · sketch: `python tools/models/props.py campanile --sketch` · rack: `--prop-rack=campanile` · review: `python tools/models/review.py campanile --round=N`

The square stone lantern on the town hall's central pavilion: a base with its cornice and the clock on its front (a stone surround, the dial with hour marks and XII, III, VI, IX painted, the hands stopped at twenty past four); the open stage of four corner piers with pilasters, a round arch on each face with its ring and imposts over a low balustrade, the bronze bell hung from its beam inside; the entablature; the dome à l'impériale in lead with ribs at its corners; a small lantern, the bronze finial, two flagstaffs leaning out of the front.
