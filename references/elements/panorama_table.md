# panorama_table — the panorama table (S231)

Family: `tools/models/props/summit.py` · registry: `scripts/models/elements.gd` · build: `tools\blender.ps1 tools\models\props.py panorama_table` · sketch: `python tools/models/props.py panorama_table --sketch` · rack: `--prop-rack=panorama_table` · review: `python tools/models/review.py panorama_table --round=N`

The orientation table at the Mirante: a round masonry drum on its plinth, cut along the slope of its bronze plate (tilted toward the visitor), a sloped coping round it; on the plate a raised rim, lines out from the centre pin to what can be seen, each named round the edge (invented: MARCHÉ, ÉGLISE, GARE, HÔPITAL, MAIRIE, CASERNE, FORÊT, BAIE, PONT; painted by position, `_paint_table`) and the N.
