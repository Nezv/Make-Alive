# tomb_column — the broken column (S189)

Family: `tools/models/props/cemetery.py` · registry: `scripts/models/elements.gd` · build: `tools\blender.ps1 tools\models\props.py tomb_column` · sketch: `python tools/models/props.py tomb_column --sketch` · rack: `--prop-rack=tomb_column` · review: `python tools/models/review.py tomb_column --round=N`

The broken column of the old cemeteries, a life cut short: two steps, a die with its plaque, a cornice, a fluted column broken off on a slant with a rough break, a stone drape falling over the break, a bronze wreath hung on the shaft.
