# shoes — shoes lost in the flight (S184)

Family: `tools/models/props/remains.py` · registry: `scripts/models/elements.gd` · build: `tools\blender.ps1 tools\models\props.py shoes` · sketch: `python tools/models/props.py shoes --sketch` · rack: `--prop-rack=shoes` · review: `python tools/models/review.py shoes --round=N`
