# police_tape — tape strung across on two stakes (S185)

Family: `tools/models/props/checkpoint.py` · registry: `scripts/models/elements.gd` · build: `tools\blender.ps1 tools\models\props.py police_tape` · sketch: `python tools/models/props.py police_tape --sketch` · rack: `--prop-rack=police_tape` · review: `python tools/models/review.py police_tape --round=N`

Red and white tape strung 4 m between two red and white stakes in rubber feet: sagging, twisting in the wind, tied round each stake, its loose end blowing off the far one. Plain bands, no words.
