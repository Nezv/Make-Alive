# antenna_mast — the guyed antenna mast (S231)

Family: `tools/models/props/summit.py` · registry: `scripts/models/elements.gd` · build: `tools\blender.ps1 tools\models\props.py antenna_mast` · sketch: `python tools/models/props.py antenna_mast --sketch` · rack: `--prop-rack=antenna_mast` · review: `python tools/models/review.py antenna_mast --round=N`

A guyed lattice mast on the summit: three galvanised legs on a concrete base, zigzag bracing and struts, the platform's ring 1.5 m under the top, three panel antennas and a dish, the cable ladder, the lightning rod, red obstruction lamps at the top and halfway (lit), guy wires from two heights to three anchor blocks 7 m out.
