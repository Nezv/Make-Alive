# coin_binoculars — the coin-operated binoculars (S231)

Family: `tools/models/props/summit.py` · registry: `scripts/models/elements.gd` · build: `tools\blender.ps1 tools\models\props.py coin_binoculars` · sketch: `python tools/models/props.py coin_binoculars --sketch` · rack: `--prop-rack=coin_binoculars` · review: `python tools/models/review.py coin_binoculars --round=N`

The viewpoint's coin binoculars: a base plate, the tapering pedestal, the yoke, and the head tilted a little up toward the view: two barrels with their hoods and glass, the eyepieces' rubber cups behind under a visor, chrome handles with black grips at the sides, the coin box with its slot and return under the head. Painted green, no maker's name.
