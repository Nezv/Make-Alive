# plane_pollard — the pollarded plane (S174)

Family: `tools/models/props/trees.py` · registry: `scripts/models/elements.gd` · build: `tools\blender.ps1 tools\models\props.py plane_pollard` · sketch: `python tools/models/props.py plane_pollard --sketch` · rack: `--prop-rack=plane_pollard` · review: `python tools/models/review.py plane_pollard --round=N`

The pollarded plane of the squares: a shorter trunk, five thick limbs cut back to knuckles, a tuft of shoots and a cluster of leaves on each knuckle.
