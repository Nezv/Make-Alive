# utility_pole — a wooden pole carrying the wires (S167)

Family: `tools/models/props/lamps.py` · registry: `scripts/models/elements.gd` · build: `tools\blender.ps1 tools\models\props.py utility_pole` · sketch: `python tools/models/props.py utility_pole --sketch` · rack: `--prop-rack=utility_pole` · review: `python tools/models/review.py utility_pole --round=N`

A wooden pole of the faubourgs carrying the overhead wires: the pole tapering, tarred black at its foot; a crossarm of four porcelain insulators on their pins with two iron braces, a smaller arm of two under it; the wires sagging off both ways; step bolts up its side; a stay wire to an anchor block.
