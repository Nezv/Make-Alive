# quay_sign — the quay's sign on its post (S202)

Family: `tools/models/props/terminal_signs.py` · registry: `scripts/models/elements.gd` · build: `tools\blender.ps1 tools\models\props.py quay_sign` · sketch: `python tools/models/props.py quay_sign --sketch` · rack: `--prop-rack=quay_sign` · review: `python tools/models/review.py quay_sign --round=N`
