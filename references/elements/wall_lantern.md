# wall_lantern — the lantern on a scrolled wall bracket (S166)

Family: `tools/models/props/lamps.py` · rack: `--prop-rack=wall_lantern` · review: `python tools/models/review.py wall_lantern --round=N`

The lamp of the old lanes where no post fits: `lamp_post`'s lantern (references, materials and rounds: `lamp_post.md`) hung by a hook and two links from an iron bracket (potence) on a house's wall: a wall plate with its bolts, the arm with its tip curling down, a brace sweeping up from the plate's foot, two scrolls filling the corner (`bury_porte_lanternes`). The house sets the wall face at z = 0 with the lantern's foot about 3.2 m over the street.
