# flagpoles — the court's three flagpoles (S222)

Family: `tools/models/props/barracks_court.py` · registry: `scripts/models/elements.gd` · build: `tools\blender.ps1 tools\models\props.py flagpoles` · sketch: `python tools/models/props.py flagpoles --sketch` · rack: `--prop-rack=flagpoles` · review: `python tools/models/review.py flagpoles --round=N`

Three white flagpoles in a row on stone bases, the middle one 10 m and the others 8 m: tapering poles, their trucks, the halyards down to cleats; a plain dark blue flag hanging limp from the middle one, folded by its own weight.
